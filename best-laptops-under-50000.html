Worth knowing before checkout

“Gaming laptop” needs a closer look.

Both picks here use integrated graphics. That keeps them affordable and portable, but it is a different class of performance from a laptop with a dedicated gaming GPU.

Online classes

Prioritise a webcam, microphone, stable Wi-Fi and a comfortable screen. Check the exact model configuration.

Casual gaming

Expect lighter titles and reduced graphics settings. Results vary by game, cooling, memory and power mode.

New AAA games

Look for a dedicated NVIDIA/AMD GPU and confirm independent performance tests. Under ₹50k, sales or compromises may be necessary.
